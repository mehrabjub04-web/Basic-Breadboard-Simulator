Board

How to place: pick a tool, then click two holes on the board (first click = lead 1 / + terminal for sources, second click = lead 2 / −).

How the board wires up: each 5-hole column in a terminal strip (A–E or F–J) is one electrical node — exactly like a real breadboard. The two power rails run the full length. Use jumper wires to bridge across the centre gap or to the rails.
